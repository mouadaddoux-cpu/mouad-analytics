Process

How an engagement runs.

Same order every time. Tracking before scaling, and a report you could take to a finance meeting.

Diagnose

Access, existing campaigns and analytics. I map what is being measured, what is missing and what the numbers currently can’t tell us. You get an audit with a short priority list.

Fix the foundation

Conversion events, naming conventions, attribution windows, the landing page. Nothing gets scaled on top of tracking that lies.

Build & test

Campaign structure, budget split, creative and audience tests. Small, readable changes with a hypothesis behind each one.

Report & iterate

A weekly one-screen read: what moved, why, and what changes next. You keep the dashboard — the account is never a black box.
