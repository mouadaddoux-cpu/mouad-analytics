Data format

MOUAD reads a single flat CSV — one row per campaign per period. The first row must be a header row; the delimiter must be a comma. Quoted fields, escaped quotes (""), a UTF-8 BOM and CRLF line endings are all handled.

Required columns

ColumnAccepted aliasesType
campaigncampaign_name, name, ad set, ad grouptext
impressionsimpression, impr, viewsinteger
clicksclick, link clicksinteger
spendcost, amount spent, budgetdecimal, currency symbol optional
conversionsconv, results, purchases, leads, ordersinteger

Optional columns

  • channel — network or platform name (Meta, Google, LinkedIn…). Shown under the campaign name.
  • week — any date or period label. Used as the x-axis of the trend chart; rows are sorted by date when the labels parse as dates.

Accepted number formats

Cells are cleaned before they are summed, so real exports can be pasted in unchanged:

1204      $1,204.50     €980       1 204
12.5%     (300)         "2,480"    1.234,56

A trailing % divides by 100, parentheses mark a negative value, and both 1,234.56 and 1.234,56 styles are detected.

How each KPI is calculated

KPIFormula
SpendΣ spend
ConversionsΣ conversions
CTRΣ clicks ÷ Σ impressions × 100
CPCΣ spend ÷ Σ clicks
Conv. rateΣ conversions ÷ Σ clicks × 100

Rates are recomputed from the summed totals rather than averaged across rows, so a tiny campaign cannot distort the account figure.

What counts as a winner

A campaign is flagged when both conditions hold on its own totals:

  • its CTR is at least 1.5× the account CTR, and
  • it has at least 100 clicks.

The click floor exists so that a four-click fluke cannot be presented as a campaign worth scaling. Both thresholds live at the top of js/app.js.

Privacy

Parsing, aggregation and charting all run in the tab. The file is read with FileReader, never posted anywhere, and nothing is persisted between sessions. The only network request the app makes is the one that loads the bundled sample CSV — and even that has an embedded fallback, so the page works fully offline.
